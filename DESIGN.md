# Design System — Connecticut House Painters

## World
Shoreline marina morning: dock-plank rhythm, salt light, painted clapboard energy. Brand colors from the incumbent CHP site; structure elevated toward NHV Painters polish.

## Palette
- Navy deep `#00152f` / ink `#062b66`
- Signal blue `#005edb` / bright `#0879f2`
- Sky `#30cfff`
- Action red `#e9090b`
- Soft ground `#f3f8ff` / foam `#e8f4ff`

## Typography
- Display: Big Shoulders Display (700/800), self-hosted
- Body: Figtree (400–700), self-hosted

## Components
- Red primary phone CTA, blue secondary phone CTA, ghost outline for secondary actions
- Sticky three-slot call bar: Office / Cell / Free estimate
- Estimate form with success state and mailto fallback
- Full-bleed photographic heroes; no card clutter in first viewport

## Motion
- Soft hero drift on background photo
- Brand rise on hero copy
- IntersectionObserver section unfolds (respects reduced motion)
