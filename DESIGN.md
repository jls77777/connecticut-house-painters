# Design System — CT Softwash LLC

## World
Working-waterfront washdown at dawn: wet deck planks, hose mist catching sunrise, harbor navy water, seafoam foam, buoy blaze as the action cleat. Willie the Whale is deckhand guide, not sticker clutter. Fun and powerful — never generic wash-card grids or AI purple / cream-terracotta clichés.

## Palette
- Harbor navy `#021828` / deep `#04253f` / water `#0a3a55`
- Seafoam `#1ec8a5` / deep `#0f9f84` / skywash `#0ea5e9`
- Foam ground `#e8f7f4` / `#d3f0ea`
- Buoy blaze `#ff5a1f` / dark `#d9420c`
- Sunrise flash `#ffc857` (focus / highlight)
- Ink `#102536` / muted `#4a6678`

## Typography
- Display: Big Shoulders Display (700/800), self-hosted — industrial boat-lettering energy
- Body: Figtree (400–700), self-hosted
- Hook for future uploads: drop `House_of_cards` / `NaveidArabic` (or brand pack fonts) into `public/fonts/` and point `--font-display` / `--font-body` here

## Components
- Buoy-orange primary phone CTA; seafoam secondary / text CTA; ghost outline on dark fields
- Sticky mobile call bar: Call / Text / Free estimate
- Estimate form with success state + Formspree or mailto fallback
- Interactive county service-area map + town SEO pages
- Full-bleed photographic heroes; brand-first first viewport; no card clutter in hero

## Motion
- Soft hero photo drift
- Brand rise on hero copy
- Willie bob (mascot presence)
- Mist wash along hero base
- IntersectionObserver section unfolds (respects reduced motion)

## Direction
Home surface contract: lobster-boat washdown dawn (seed `9e96846b`, grounded candidate 7). Raises: monumental brand type + calm CTA space; narrative service panels; Willie expressive guide; buoy-orange accent share.
